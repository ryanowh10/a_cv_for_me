Project notes

Agentic AI Camp — YDSP
At the Agentic AI Camp, I explored the development and practical applications of agentic artificial intelligence. Unlike conventional chatbots that primarily respond to individual prompts, agentic AI systems can plan tasks, use external tools and execute multi-step workflows with greater autonomy. I set up and experimented with OpenClaw, using it to develop AI agents capable of automating processes and carrying out tasks with limited human intervention. A key part of the project involved understanding how an agent could break down a larger objective into smaller actions, generate and execute code where necessary, and iteratively work towards a desired outcome. The project also explored how AI agents can interact through messaging platforms, demonstrating how an AI system can extend beyond a standalone interface and become part of an automated workflow. Through the camp, I gained hands-on experience with LLM-based agents, tool calling, workflow automation, prompt design, debugging and AI-generated code. More importantly, the project gave me practical insight into both the capabilities and limitations of autonomous AI systems, particularly the importance of providing agents with appropriate tools, constraints and verification mechanisms.
